Advance only the explicit roots and selected descendants, through the requested maximum phase. Earlier phases may be necessary; a later phase requires a separate instruction. Explore covers reading and empirical probes; plan covers investigation conclusions and reviewed proposals; work covers implementation; review adds independent candidate review; integrate adds configured host integration. Do not silently bypass an earlier unresolved requirement to reach the phase limit.

Choose the next actionable step using current evidence. For a defect, preserve the observed-versus-expected behavior and reproduce before proposing a correction. Explore plausible causes and discriminating observations; record hypothesis branches and empirical research where useful. Refer requirement/preference choices to the user as Questions recorded before you stop, never as prose alone. An item BlockedBy an Open Question waits for its answer. Before you dispatch work that a Question gated, read its answer: an Answered Question releases the work only as far as the answer allows. When the answer refuses the work, do not dispatch it: cancel it or leave it blocked, and tell the operator. Carry a condition the answer sets into the requirements of the work, or ask it in a follow-up Question. A Withdrawn Question never releases the work: Produce a new Question and link the gated items BlockedBy it, or remove the link and record the reason. Do not ask for a go-ahead that the invocation already gives. Record evidence-supported conclusions and decisions, then plan bounded work. A Task without an Open milestone is not implemented: link it to an Open milestone as the shared rules describe, and do not create a milestone yourself. Retain handoffs when another actor or environment must supply the next step. Before the first Planner Select for the roots, search ledger:Memories status:Current with terms from the roots and pass the applicable Memories as guidance. Apply a Memory only from a reviewed proposal that states its applicability and cites its evidence.

Forward evidence, plans, candidates and rejected-review handles directly to the appropriate role. Apply an accepted plan by its stored handle. Implement and validate in the assigned workspace. When a worker result's host check failed and its retained output shows a failure of the check's environment rather than of the candidate, dispatch Revalidate for that result instead of a worker: the host reruns the failed checks on the same commit, within the configured rounds, and a passing round lets review and integration proceed with the unchanged result. A failure the candidate caused goes to a worker. For ChangesRequested, preserve the finding, send its handle to the next worker/planner, and obtain a fresh independent review of the corrected identity. A review of old item revisions does not approve current work. Workers run focused tests. When the project configures checks, the host runs them on each candidate and again on the commit that lands when the target has moved. Never dispatch a child to run a configured check, a whole-project suite or a delivery gate, except a Worker Probe whose hypothesis is about that command, and do not treat the absence of such a run from a worker's report as a failure.

Within the integrate phase, use the configured integration target and existing prepare/status/preview/apply/discard operations. When the target has moved, prepare again under a fresh identity: the host rebases the reviewed candidate onto the new head and reruns the configured checks, and a preview without a blocker integrates as it is. Only a preview whose blocker reports a conflict, a failed check or a refused rebase requires combination and fresh validation/review. Without an integration target, retain the reviewed candidate and report that incorporation remains outstanding. Re-establish current worker/planner results and review when continuing an older session's work; historical handles do not carry this session's write authority.

Close a Defect yourself once its work is integrated. When a Defect has at least one produced Task, every Task produced from it is Done by recorded integration and each states that its integration resolves the Defect, change the Defect with Replace: keep the rest of its draft, set status Resolved and append one resolution entry of ModelDeclared origin that names those Tasks and cites the integrated commit from each Task's validation evidence. Send the Defect's current revision, and its fence while you hold a claim on it. The ledger refuses a HostObserved or HumanReported entry you add. A Defect with no produced Task, such as one recorded only because it blocks other items, is not closed this way. While a driver is on, the Defect must be a selected member of the drive: leave a Defect outside the drive's set Open and report it, because that write is refused as an out-of-set change and stops the drive with Failure. Use a Planner and Plan review to close a Defect only when one of its Tasks names a further observation; obtain that observation first.

Report observed progress per member, unresolved blockers and omitted/unexamined work. Preserve failed/cancelled attempts and their spend. Never mark all grouped work successful from one passing shared check.
