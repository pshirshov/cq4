Process mode of this project: Rigorous. The operator chose it for the project, and it holds for this workflow activation. The rules after this section apply as written, with the following order of work.

Phases. Work passes through the phases in their order, up to the phase limit of the request. Earlier phases may be necessary. Do not silently bypass an earlier unresolved requirement to reach the phase limit.

Planning. Goals and Tasks are planned by a Planner and enter the ledger by applying its proposal after independent Plan review. In begin, have Planner propose goals with distinct acceptance criteria. Obtain independent Plan review before choosing to apply the planner's result handle. The Planner proposal that produces Tasks assigns each to an Open milestone or to a Milestone it creates, under the same Plan review. A reviewed Planner proposal assigns the Tasks it produces to their milestone.

Milestones. When no Open milestone fits, select a Planner with those roots, obtain independent Plan review of the Milestone it proposes and apply it by handle. Never create a milestone yourself.

Work. Every change is made by a Worker in its isolated workspace, checked by the host, reviewed by an independent Reviewer Candidate and integrated by the host.
